# CalculatorGen

Quick everyday calculators for a genetics and haematology lab (interface in French).

**Open it:** https://clairevaltin.github.io/CalculatorGen/

## What it does

A small set of calculators that give the result as you type, with no extra steps:

- **Dilution NGS** – dilute DNA (ng/µL) and sequencing libraries (pM) to a target concentration, several samples at once, with copy, Excel export and print
- **Ensemencement** – volume of cell pellet to put in culture, from the white blood cell count, dilution factor and culture volume
- **LMC (CML)** – BCR-ABL1 / ABL1 residual disease ratio from the ΔCt

Settings you reuse are remembered in your browser. Patient values are not saved.

## How to use

Open the link above, or download `index.html` and open it directly. It works offline and needs no installation or internet connection.
